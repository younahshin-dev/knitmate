Round Coaster (Crochet, US terms, worked in rounds)
Original sample pattern for KnitMate demo. Free to use.

Materials: Worsted weight cotton yarn, about 10 g. 4.0 mm crochet hook.

Make a magic ring.
Rnd 1: Ch 1 (does not count as st), 6 sc in ring, sl st to first sc to join. (6 sts)
Rnd 2: Ch 1, 2 sc in each st around, sl st to first sc. (12 sts)
Rnd 3: Ch 1, *sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around, sl st to first sc. (18 sts)
Rnd 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; rep from * around, sl st to first sc. (24 sts)
Rnd 5: Ch 3 (counts as dc), dc in same st, dc in next 3 sts, *2 dc in next st, dc in next 3 sts; rep from * around, sl st to top of beginning ch-3. (30 sts)
Fasten off and weave in ends.
